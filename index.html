Math

How the number is built

Flip

Max offer = ARV − repairs − holding − buy close − sell close − target profit. Go needs profit and at least 15% ROI on cash in.

Wholesale

Buyer max = ARV − repairs − buyer profit − close/hold. Your contract price is that number minus your assignment fee.

Rental

NOI after vacancy and expenses. Cash-on-cash uses down payment. DSCR is NOI divided by yearly mortgage. Max price hunts for 8% CoC and 1.25 DSCR.

Pallet and service

Pallet profit is live units times sell price, minus buy and fees. Service quote is labor plus marked-up materials plus trip cost.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?
On this device, in localStorage. Clearing site data wipes the board. Export JSON or CSV anytime.
How is max offer calculated on a flip?
ARV minus repairs, holding, buy close, sell close, and your target profit. That leftover is the most you should pay.
What does the score mean?
0–100. Under 60 is a no-go. It mixes profit, ROI or cash-on-cash, and cushion versus a simple rule of thumb. It is not a guarantee.
Is this financial advice?
No. It is a calculator. You still verify comps, repairs, rent, and title.
Can I edit a saved deal?
Yes. Click any deal card. It loads back into the analyzer so you can tweak and re-save.
