# Deal Forge

Live site: https://bet34.github.io/deal-forge-website/

Browser tool for flips, wholesale, rentals, pallet lots, and service jobs.

- Max offer + 0–100 deal score
- Hard kill rules and watch warnings
- Repair contingency pad on flips and wholesale
- Hard money points and rate, subtracted from the flip max offer
- Partner split: your take vs partner take
- Cash to close, break-even sell, profit per day
- Offer ladder: open 92%, target 96%, walk 100%
- Base vs +10% haircut check
- Michigan close-cost fill on flips (state $3.75 per $500 + typical county $0.55)
- County picker: Wayne, Oakland, Macomb, Monroe, or state tax only
- Short text you can copy and send
- Seller asking vs max, with room or overage bar
- 70% rule check and annualized flip ROI
- Stress haircut on ARV, rent, or sell price
- Downriver presets (Taylor flip, Wyandotte flip, wholesale, returns lot, furnace job, Southgate rental)
- Copy-ready offer letter, ladder, and share link
- Pipeline with stage filter, sort, age, stale flag, duplicate, compare top two
- Closed profit and open cash on the board
- JSON import + export, CSV export

Data stays in the browser. Not financial advice. Michigan close fill uses state transfer tax plus a typical county $0.55 per $500. Confirm the county before you wire. Agent commission is not included.
